2. Language & Layout Switching

The portal features a zero-reload language toggle in the top-right corner of the navigation bar on every page.

  • Switch Button: Labeled العربية when in English mode, and English when in Arabic mode.
  • Instant Toggle: Updates all text, headers, table columns, badges, and modals immediately without refreshing the page.
  • Right-to-Left (RTL) Typography: Switching to Arabic activates full right-to-left layout alignment and renders typography using the Google Cairo font.
  • Session Persistence: Your language choice is saved in your browser storage and persists across visits.

3. Patient Portal Walkthrough

1. Dashboard (/patient/dashboard)

The main overview screen displays your next confirmed doctor visit, your active medication list, and recent notifications from your care team.

2. Booking an Appointment (/patient/appointments)

  1. Click "Book appointment" in the top navigation or on the appointments page.
  2. Choose your attending doctor from the physician directory.
  3. Select your preferred consultation date and time.
  4. Type your consultation reason (e.g. "Routine blood pressure follow-up").
  5. Click "Confirm Booking". The appointment status displays as Pending until confirmed by clinic staff.

3. Electronic Medical Records (/patient/records)

  1. Click "Medical Records" in the sidebar.
  2. Filter by category: Lab Results, Visit Summaries, or Diagnoses.
  3. Review findings, clinical notes, and attending doctor remarks.
  4. Click "Export as Text" to download a formatted clinical document.

4. Prescriptions & Medications (/patient/prescriptions)

  1. Click "Prescriptions" in the sidebar.
  2. Toggle between Active Prescriptions and Past Medications.
  3. Check exact dosage (e.g. 10mg tablet), dosing frequency (e.g. Once daily with meals), treatment dates, and doctor instructions.

5. Personal Profile & Emergency Contacts (/patient/profile)

Keep your contact telephone, home address, medical insurance carrier ID, and emergency contact details updated. Changes save immediately to your record.

4. Staff Clinical Workspace Walkthrough

1. Today's Clinical Queue (/staff/dashboard)

Real-time triage queue for all visits scheduled for the current day. Clinicians can manage appointments with one click:

  • Confirm: Validates and confirms pending patient appointment requests.
  • Complete: Marks completed consultations.
  • Cancel: Cancels appointment slots.
  • Open Patient Chart: Opens the patient's electronic health record directly from any appointment row.

2. Patient Directory (/staff/patients)

Searchable register across all enrolled patients. Filter by patient name, email, or telephone number to view demographics and open patient charts.

3. Electronic Health Chart (/staff/patients/[id])

  • Demographics Card: Contact details, date of birth, insurance carrier, policy ID, emergency contacts, and home address.
  • Add Medical Record: Click "Add record" to author lab reports, visit summaries, or diagnostic findings. Supports attaching documents (PDF, PNG, JPG).
  • Issue Prescription: Click "Add prescription" to enter medication name, dosage, daily frequency, start/end dates, and pharmacy instructions.
  • Appointment History: View full consultation history and update statuses directly.
